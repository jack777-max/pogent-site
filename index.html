Half launchpad, half agent

The name says it: Pons + Agent = Pogent. One side is the fair-launch format that made Pons the home of Robinhood Chain launches. The other side is an autonomous agent that does the watching and the legwork.

A fair launch is only fair if someone is paying attention. Launches move fast: contracts appear, liquidity locks, the book fills in minutes. Most people find out late — the agent finds out first.

Pogent is being built as that agent. It monitors Robinhood Chain around the clock, prepares launch steps ahead of time, and keeps a verifiable on-chain record of everything it touches — no hidden allocations, no quiet changes, everything readable on-chain.

Pogent is an original, independent project. It takes the launch experience people already know from Pons and gives it a tireless operator.

What the agent does

Four jobs, running continuously — the same jobs a sharp launch team does by hand, done by an agent instead.

Watches every launch

The agent scans Robinhood Chain for new launches as they happen — new contracts, fresh liquidity, opening books — and flags what matters the moment it lands on-chain.

Prepares the launch

Before a launch goes live, the agent lines up the steps: contract checks, parameters, and the launch sequence — prepared in advance, not improvised under pressure.

Tracks the book on-chain

After launch, the agent follows the book directly from chain data — trades, liquidity, and holder movement — so the picture you get is the chain's, not a rumor's.

Always on

Markets on Robinhood Chain don't keep office hours, and neither does the agent. It watches, prepares, and tracks continuously — today, and every day after.
